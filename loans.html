Loan products

Credit sized against your shares and savings, with transparent, Board-reviewed terms.

Ordinary Loan

Our standard, longer-term member loan.

  • Sized against shares, savings and repayment capacity
  • Rate and maximum term set by the Board, reviewed periodically
  • Guarantor and/or collateral required depending on amount

Emergency Loan

Faster access for urgent, unplanned needs.

  • Simplified appraisal for smaller amounts
  • Shorter repayment period
  • Still subject to eligibility and guarantor checks

Express Loan

Quicker turnaround for established members.

  • Streamlined for members with a clean repayment history
  • Capped at a lower maximum amount

Weekend Loan

Short-term credit released ahead of the weekend.

  • Very short repayment window
  • Modest amounts, fast processing

Exact interest rates, maximum amounts and fees for each product are set and periodically reviewed by the Board. Contact the Savings & Credit Officer for current figures.

Eligibility

Who can apply

  • Fully paid-up member
  • 18 years or older, of sound mind
  • An active shareholder with appropriate shareholding
  • No outstanding loan of the same product
  • Good credit history
  • Guarantors who are not themselves defaulters
How it works

From application to disbursement

  • Apply

    Submit your loan application with the product, amount and purpose.

  • Appraisal

    The Savings & Credit Officer reviews your shares, savings, existing loans and repayment capacity.

  • Guarantors & security

    Guarantors confirm their commitment; any required collateral is verified.

  • Approval

    Management approves within its limit, or the Credit Committee decides for larger amounts — with Credit Committee ratification either way.

  • Agreement & disbursement

    You sign the loan agreement, and funds are disbursed to your savings account.
