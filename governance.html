Who does what

Governance bodies

Annual General Meeting

The AGM is the supreme governing body of SODIS-SACCO, made up of fully paid-up members. It receives the Board's report, approves audited accounts and the coming year's budget, and elects leadership.

The Board (Executive Committee)

Elected by members to manage and supervise SODIS-SACCO on their behalf, and to report back to members through the AGM.

Supervisory Board

Performs the SACCO's internal audit role — verifying loans, savings and share accounts, reviewing compliance, and following up on audit findings and AGM/committee resolutions.

Board Sub-Committees

Finance & Risk, Management & Operations, Lending & Credit, and Investment, Partnerships & Research committees, each with defined responsibilities reporting to the Board.

Meetings

How governance meets

  • Annual General Meeting

    Held annually, with at least 14 days' notice, to review performance and approve accounts and budgets.

  • Board meetings

    Held quarterly, with invitations sent seven days in advance along with the agenda, minutes and management reports.

  • Committee meetings

    Sub-committees meet as required to review matters within their mandate before reporting to the Board.

  • Supervisory reviews

    The Supervisory Board conducts audit and inspection work on an unscheduled basis, following up on prior recommendations.
