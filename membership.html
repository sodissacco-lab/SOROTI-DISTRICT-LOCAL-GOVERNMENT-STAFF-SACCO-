Become a member

Membership is open to Soroti District Local Government staff. Apply online — a Membership Officer will follow up on verification.

How it works

From application to membership number

  • Apply online

    Complete the form below with your personal, employment and next-of-kin details.

  • Verification

    The Membership Relations Officer checks your details and employment status.

  • Review & approval

    The General Manager reviews and approves the application.

  • Membership number issued

    You'll be contacted with your membership number and account setup instructions.
