Savings products

Ways to grow your money safely with SODIS-SACCO, at rates reviewed periodically by the Board.

Ordinary Savings Account

Your everyday SACCO account.

  • Deposit and withdraw as needed
  • Interest calculated quarterly on your minimum balance
  • Low minimum opening amount, set by the Board
  • A small monthly/quarterly maintenance charge applies

Target / Contractual Savings

Save toward a specific goal.

  • Higher interest than an ordinary account
  • No withdrawal until the target date or amount is reached
  • Ideal for planned expenses — school fees, land, construction

Fixed / Term Deposit

Lock in a rate for a fixed period.

  • Minimum UGX 1,000,000
  • Term from 3 to 12 months
  • Interest paid on maturity
  • Early withdrawal forfeits interest earned
How interest works

Interest on your savings

Ordinary accounts

Interest is calculated quarterly on your minimum balance for that quarter, and credited to your account quarterly. All interest calculations use a 365-day year.

Fixed deposits

Interest accrues daily but is credited on the terms agreed when you open the deposit — typically at maturity. Rates reflect prevailing market conditions and are reviewed periodically by the Board.

Good to know

Keeping your account active

An account with no member-initiated transaction for 12 months is classified as dormant and flagged in our system. You'll be notified in writing, and reactivating simply requires a written request. Interest and standard SACCO charges don't count as activity for this purpose.
