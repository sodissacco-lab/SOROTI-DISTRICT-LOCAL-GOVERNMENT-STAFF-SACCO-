Our core values

What guides every decision we make

Accountability

Accountable to our members, the Government and all our stakeholders, individually and collectively.

Member orientation

Our products and services are member-driven, built to satisfy diverse and changing needs.

Transparency

Open dealings with every stakeholder, and the same sincerity expected in return.

Professionalism

Impeccable standards in the conduct of our affairs, and effective service to every member.

Integrity

A high degree of honesty in serving members and every other stakeholder.

Teamwork

Close collaboration between members, the Executive Committee, Supervisory Board and staff.
