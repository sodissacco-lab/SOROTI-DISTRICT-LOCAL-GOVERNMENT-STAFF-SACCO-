About SODIS-SACCO

Established in 2024 and registered under 9020/RCS, drawing membership from Soroti District Local Government staff.

Who we are

SODIS-SACCO is the Soroti District Local Government Staff Cooperative Savings and Credit Society Limited — a member-owned financial cooperative built to mobilize savings and extend affordable credit to district staff.

Every member is also an owner: decisions are made through the Annual General Meeting, the Board, and the Supervisory Board, on behalf of the people the SACCO serves.

Our vision

"Promoting member prosperity for generations as a leading SACCO."

Our mission

To mobilize financial resources and provide affordable financial services to members.

Our objectives

What SODIS-SACCO exists to do

  • Provide savings and credit services to members
  • Offer development financing
  • Improve members' economic and social conditions
  • Promote a culture of savings
  • Offer competitive savings and deposit services
  • Provide sustainable loan services
  • Support responsible loan recovery
  • Pursue development and investment projects
  • Maintain professional SACCO management
  • Offer other legally authorized, economically feasible financial services
Core values

The standards behind our name

Accountability

To members, government and every stakeholder, individually and collectively.

Member orientation

Products and services designed around real, changing member needs.

Transparency

Open, sincere dealing at every level of the SACCO.

Professionalism

Impeccable standards in the conduct of our affairs.

Integrity

A high degree of honesty toward members and stakeholders.

Teamwork

Close collaboration among members, Executive Committee, Supervisory Board and staff.

SODIS-SACCO also encourages respect for diversity among members, the Board, committees and management in fulfilling the SACCO's mandate.
